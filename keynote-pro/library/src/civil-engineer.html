Design basis

What the structure must carry

Loads

  • Pedestrian crowd: 5.0 kN/m2
  • Maintenance vehicle: 7.5 t
  • Wind on deck and parapet

Limits

  • Deflection under 1/800 of span
  • Vertical frequency above 3.4 Hz
  • Design life 100 years

The frequency limit avoids pedestrian resonance; it governs more than strength does.

Utilisation by member

Every member is under 90 percent; the bottom flange governs.

Utilisation is demand over capacity. Anything over 100 percent fails; we keep a 10 percent reserve for design changes.

Materials

Weathering steel avoids repainting over the canal. Timber is certified and replaceable by plank.
