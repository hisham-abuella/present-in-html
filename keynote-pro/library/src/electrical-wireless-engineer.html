Every doubling of distance costs 6 dB.

Free space, any frequency. Walls, foliage and bodies come on top.

The rule of thumb that anchors the rest of the review: 20 log10 of 2 is about 6.02 dB.

Link budget

Add the gains, subtract the loss

Pr = Pt + Gt + Gr − Lfs
Pt
Transmit power, 43 dBm
Gt
Antenna gain, 17 dBi
Gr
Handset gain, 0 dBi
Lfs
Free-space loss, 89.3 dB at 200 m

L = 20 log10(d in km) + 20 log10(f in MHz) + 32.44. At 0.2 km and 3500 MHz that is -14.0 + 70.9 + 32.4 = 89.3 dB.

Loss grows 6 dB per doubling

From 77 dB at 50 m to 101 dB at 800 m.

Each point is the previous plus 6.0 dB. Real urban loss is steeper; this is the best case.
