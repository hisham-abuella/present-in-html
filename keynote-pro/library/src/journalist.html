The empty ferry

Six years, $41 million, and a route that carries almost no one: a data-led investigation.

A subsidised ferry carried 11 passengers a day.

The nut graf in one sentence. If the room is not interested after this, the pitch is dead.

What we know

$41M

In public subsidy since 2020, about $11,900 per rider per year.

Source: city budget filings, ridership counts obtained under public records law

Show the math: 41 million over six years, 11 riders a day. Check that math again before publishing.

Claim, evidence, caveat

  2. Operator logs, two former drivers on the record, city audit draft.
