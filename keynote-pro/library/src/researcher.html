Compound X lowers IL-6 release in a dose-dependent way, with no loss of viability.

State the result first; the audience then judges the evidence.

Methods

Four steps, three replicates

  1. Culture

    Macrophage-like cells, passage 6 to 9.

  2. Dose

    0, 1, 5, 25 micromolar, 2 hours.

  3. Stimulate

    LPS for 24 hours.

  4. Measure

    IL-6 by ELISA, viability by MTT.

Three independent experiments, each in triplicate wells.

IL-6 falls as the dose rises

Half the control level at 25 micromolar.
