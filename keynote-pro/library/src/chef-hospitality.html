Fictional restaurant and dishes. Open by saying the briefing takes twelve minutes and ends with a tasting at the pass.

The idea

Cook what is in season, within thirty miles, and say so on the card.

Eight of ten dishes now come from three named farms.

The whole menu hangs on this sentence. Servers should be able to say it to a guest unprompted.

Four courses, four changes

  • To begin: roasted squash soup, brown butter, sage.
  • Fish: pan-fried hake, leeks, cider cream.
  • Main: slow-braised shoulder of lamb, white beans, gremolata.
  • To finish: baked quince, oat crumble, clotted cream.

Walk the card in order. Mention allergens as you go: dairy in all four, gluten in the finish, fish in the second.

Plates sold per service

The hake outsold every other dish at Friday dinner.

Hake is the dish to guide guests toward when they ask for a recommendation. Prep for sixty.

How a table moves through service

  1. Greet

    Water, card, and the story in one line.

  2. Order

    Allergies first, then courses.

  3. Fire

    Ticket to the pass, mains timed to starters.

  4. Finish

    Dessert wine offered before the plate.

The only new step is the allergy question before the first course. Everything else is how you already work.
