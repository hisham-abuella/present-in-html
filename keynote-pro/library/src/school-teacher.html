How plants make food

A 30-minute lesson on photosynthesis.

Ms. Sample, Room 12Sample lessonLesson 4

Sample lesson. Ask: where does a tree get its food? Take three answers before moving on.

Today we will

  1. Find the ingredients 5 min
  2. Follow the recipe 10 min
  3. Check our thinking 5 min
  4. Draw it ourselves 10 min

Read the four steps aloud with the class.

Plants make their own food from light, water and air.

Pause here. Let children say it back in their own words.

The recipe

Light, water and air in. Sugar and oxygen out.

Three clicks: ingredients, the leaf as kitchen, then the two things that come out.

Check your understanding

Which gas do plants take in for photosynthesis?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Carbon dioxide
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Helium

Vote with fingers (1 to 4) first, then click to reveal B. Ask a child who chose A to explain their idea kindly.

Remember

  • Plants are producers. They make their own food.
  • Leaves are the kitchen.
  • Oxygen is a by-product that we breathe.

Three sentences for the book.
