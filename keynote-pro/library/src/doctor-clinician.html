Differential

Four causes we could not ignore

Fits

  • Acute coronary syndrome: pressure, radiation, risk factors
  • Aortic dissection: hypertension, severe pain

Less likely

  • Pulmonary embolism: no leg signs, normal oxygenation
  • Pericarditis: no positional change

Each has a must-not-miss feature; the ECG and the troponin trend sort them.

Troponin rose over six hours

A rising pattern, well above the 99th percentile of 14 ng/L.

Rise and fall is what separates acute injury from chronic elevation.

Take-home points

  • ECG early, repeat it if pain changes.
  • Trend the troponin; one value is a snapshot.
  • Hold the dissection thought until you have excluded it.
