Analysis

Issue, rule, application, conclusion

  1. Does clause 14.2 protect a legitimate interest, and go no further than necessary?

  2. A restraint is enforceable only if reasonable between the parties and in the public interest.

  3. The interest is genuine. The territory and the ban on any role are wider than that interest requires.

  4. Probably unenforceable as drafted

    A narrower clause would likely be upheld.

The memo's spine. Be clear it is the preliminary view.

The arguments, side by side

For enforcing

  • Employee held confidential pricing data
  • Twelve months is not unusual
  • Signed with independent advice

Against enforcing

  • Territory covers markets the employer never served
  • Bars any role, not only competing ones
  • No garden-leave payment
