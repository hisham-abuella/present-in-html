Finding

People did not abandon at payment. They gave up at the address form.

Six of eight stalled there for more than 40 seconds.

Counter the team's assumption that payment was the problem. Show the session clip if time allows.

Time on each step

The address step takes longer than payment and confirmation together.

Eight participants is a small sample; we are reading patterns, not percentages.

Insights

Observation, cause, change

  1. Participants retyped their street twice when autofill filled the wrong field.

  2. Fields are named in a non-standard order, so the browser cannot map them.

  3. Use standard field names and a single search-as-you-type address line.

  4. About 30 seconds saved

    To be tested in the next round.

Expected saving is an estimate from the sessions, not a measured result.

Crit this

Two flows to compare

Flow A: one page

  • Everything on a single scroll
  • Fewer taps, longer page
  • Risk: feels heavy on small phones

Flow B: three steps

  • Address, delivery, pay
  • Progress bar always visible
  • Risk: back button confusion

Ask the room to look at the happy path first, then the error states. Park visual polish for later.
