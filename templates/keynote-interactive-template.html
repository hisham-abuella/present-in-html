YouTube Embed

Video Embed Code

<!-- Local/Remote Video -->
<video controls width="100%">
  <source src="video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

<!-- YouTube Embed -->
<iframe src="https://youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID"></iframe>

<!-- Auto-play on slide (muted required) -->
<video data-autoplay loop muted playsinline>
  <source src="animation.mp4">
</video>

Video Background

Add video as slide background:

<section
  data-background-video="video.mp4"
  data-background-video-loop
  data-background-video-muted>
</section>
